# Provenance

Copilot Stack is an independently authored, unofficial personal toolkit. It is
not a Microsoft/GitHub/Meta product or an endorsed distribution of those brands.

Garry Tan's MIT-licensed gstack informed the lifecycle and role approach. Checklist
Design, Amicro, Beautiful UI, AIforUI, Astryx, Transitions.dev and SVGL were reviewed
as requested references. Their code/skills/assets are not redistributed here.
Adopted future app code/assets must retain their applicable notices and satisfy
the exact source/provider terms. See docs/SOURCES.md for verified sources and
docs/DESIGN-RESOURCES.md for the roles and limitations.
