# Prepare a Microsoft 365 app handoff

Identify the reader and the decision or action they own. Gather the product
brief, changed behavior, tested revision, environment, known issues and release
decision from available sources or pasted material. Use a Teams update for a
short coordination request, a Word brief for a durable decision, or an Outlook
draft for a named recipient. Follow the user's requested destination and tone.

In a session without code or connector tools, work in planning-only mode from
the supplied sources. Produce copy-ready material in the conversation and
identify missing evidence. Never infer permission to read a private channel,
change an app, send a message or update a shared document from the presence of
this skill.

## Translate evidence for the reader

Lead with the user-visible change and current release state. Name who can try
it, where, and how they can recognize success. Describe a blocker by its effect
on the intended user. Give the responsible person one concrete action and an
existing deadline if provided. Leave unassigned work visibly unassigned.
Preserve source wording for adopted decisions and identify proposals separately.

Attach the smallest supporting reference for each consequential claim. A passing
local test supports the named behavior in that environment. A tenant smoke
result can support the named host, tenant and identity. Confirm a rollout or
announcement only from an observed release or delivery record. Avoid including
access tokens, raw customer records, unrelated transcript excerpts or internal
links the audience cannot open. If sources disagree, cite the disagreement and
identify the decision owner.

## Verify the deliverable

For a draft, reread it as the recipient and check that the next action, owner
and evidence are findable. For an authorized document update, inspect the
destination and preserve existing author content, then reopen the updated
section and verify its location. For an explicitly authorized send, confirm
recipients and the resulting delivery reference in the tool. An accessible
source document grants no implied authority to widen its sharing.

Return the final brief or message, source references and remaining decisions.
State whether it was drafted, saved, or sent, based on the observed result. If a
connector was unavailable, deliver the complete text and identify the exact
pending destination action.
